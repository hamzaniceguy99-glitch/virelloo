How we work

A coach who sells no clothes

Most style content exists to sell you something. The advice bends toward whatever pays a commission, and you end up with more clothes and the same problem.

Virelloo earns nothing from anything you buy. We start with your closet, aim to make you buy less, and consider the job done when getting dressed stops taking up mental space.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Do you tell me what to buy?

Only after the audit, and usually it's three or four pieces. We take no affiliate commissions and recommend no particular retailer.

Is this only for women?

No. Roughly a third of clients are men, and the method — fit, proportion, formulas — is the same.

I have a small budget. Is it worth it?

That's often when it helps most: the aim is to stop spending on clothes that don't get worn. Thrift and secondhand work fine with this approach.

Do I have to get rid of things?

No. We separate, store and revisit. Nothing leaves your home unless you decide it should.

What about body changes?

We dress the body you have now. If your size is changing, we build a smaller, flexible rotation instead of a full wardrobe.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contract, no minimum term. Access stays open until the end of the month you already paid for.

Tired of a full closet and nothing to wear?

Twenty minutes on what's not working and where to start. Free, and nobody sells you anything.
