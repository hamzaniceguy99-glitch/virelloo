The programs, in detail

Four programs of 6 to 10 weeks, all starting from the clothes you already own.

Program 01 · Stop buying mistakes

Find your style

"Classic with an edge" helps nobody in a fitting room. We build a style description precise enough to be useful: the shapes that suit you, the colors you reach for, and the life you actually dress for — school run and video calls included, not a fantasy version.

  • 6 weeks
  • Any starting point
  • 2 hours a week
  • A phone camera

Start this program

Weeks 1–2 · What you like

Collecting references and finding the pattern in them.

Weeks 3–4 · What suits you

Proportion, shape and color, tested on you rather than in theory.

Weeks 5–6 · Your description

A written style brief you can shop and dress by.

Weeks 1–2 · Everything out

Photographing and sorting the whole closet, honestly.

Weeks 3–4 · The keep pile

What fits, what suits you, what a tailor can rescue.

Weeks 5–6 · Formulas

Five outfit templates you can repeat without thinking.

Weeks 7–8 · The gaps

The few pieces genuinely worth buying, and nothing else.

Program 02 · Use what you own

Wardrobe audit

Most closets hold three wardrobes: the one you wear, the one you're saving, and the one that was a mistake. We separate them, work out which pieces earn their space, and build a handful of outfit formulas so getting dressed stops being a daily decision.

  • 8 weeks
  • Any starting point
  • 3 hours a week
  • Your existing closet

Start this program

Program 03 · Buy less, better

Shopping & fit

Shopping badly is expensive and demoralizing. We practice judging fit at the shoulder and the waist, reading a fabric label, and testing a purchase against the outfits you already own. We recommend no brands and earn nothing from anything you buy.

  • 6 weeks
  • Any budget
  • 2 hours a week
  • Access to any store

Start this program

Weeks 1–2 · Fit

Shoulders, rise, break: the three checks that matter most.

Weeks 3–4 · Quality

Fabric, seams and what's worth paying for at your budget.

Weeks 5–6 · The rules

A short list that stops impulse buys, tested on a real trip.

Weeks 1–2 · Work

A rotation for your actual workplace, on camera and in person.

Weeks 3–4 · Big moments

Interviews, presentations, photographs.

Weeks 5–6 · Events and travel

Dress codes decoded, and packing that stays small.

Program 04 · Dress for the room

Work & events

The stressful outfits are the ones with a deadline. We plan them before the week they're needed: what reads well on camera, what works for a specific dress code, and how to pack for a trip without three unworn options.

  • 6 weeks
  • Any starting point
  • 2 hours a week
  • Your calendar
